YOUR FIRST AI HUMAN — NO TECHNICAL SETUP REQUIRED

You do not need Terminal, Python, PowerShell, Git, GitHub, or VS Code.

1. Keep one untouched copy of this starter folder.
2. Make a duplicate and rename the duplicate for one job.
3. Open the ChatGPT desktop app and sign in.
4. Choose Codex.
5. Open Projects and add the duplicated folder as a local project.
6. Start New chat.
7. Ask Codex: "Read START-HERE.md and walk me through it one click at a time. I am a complete beginner."

Mac folders are created in Finder: Documents > File > New Folder.
Windows folders are created in File Explorer: Documents > New > Folder.
